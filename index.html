Custom stitching

Every size from S to XXXXL is stitched in-house, and any design can be made to your measurements. Send us a photo of what you want, or pick something from the collection and tell us what to change.

Start a custom order

  1. Share the design

    Send a photo, a sketch or a product from this site on WhatsApp.

  2. Send your measurements

    We tell you exactly which measurements we need and how to take them.

  3. We cut and stitch

    Your outfit is cut and stitched by our own tailors. We confirm the price and date before we start.

  4. Collect or get it delivered

    Pick it up from us or we arrange delivery. We confirm on WhatsApp when it is ready.
